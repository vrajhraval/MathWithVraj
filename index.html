✨ The Magic of 9

Multiply any number by 9, and add up the individual digits of the answer. They will always reduce back to 9! Try it: \(9 \times 23 = 207 \rightarrow 2+0+7 = 9\).

🍕 The Pizza Equation

If you have a pizza with radius z and thickness a, its volume can be calculated perfectly as: $$\text{Volume} = \pi \times z \times z \times a = \text{PIZZA}$$
