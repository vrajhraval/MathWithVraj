The Evolution of Mathematics

   

Journey through the history of logic and numbers

   
       

1. Ancient Foundations

       
           
               

                    The Babylonians and Egyptians used mathematics for taxation,                     astronomy, engineering, and architecture.                

           
                   
   
   
       

2. Greek & Indian Contributions

       
                       
               

                    From the geometric proofs of Pythagoras to the invention                     of Zero in India, these civilizations laid                     the foundation of modern mathematics.
