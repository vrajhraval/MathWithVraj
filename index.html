The Evolution of Mathematics

Journey through the history of logic, numbers and mathematical thinking

1. Ancient Foundations

Mathematics has been an essential part of human civilisation since ancient times.

The Babylonians and Egyptians used mathematics for taxation, astronomy, engineering, construction, agriculture and architecture.

Their practical mathematical ideas became the foundation for later developments in geometry, arithmetic and algebra.

🏺 📐 ∑

2. Greek & Indian Contributions

🏛️ 0️⃣ ∞

Greek mathematicians developed rigorous geometric reasoning and proof.

From the geometric ideas associated with Pythagoras to the remarkable development of zero and place-value mathematics in India, different civilisations shaped mathematics into a powerful logical discipline.
