The Fibonacci Sequence

Where Math Meets Nature, Art, and Science

The Fibonacci sequence is a beautiful mathematical pattern where each number is the sum of the two preceding ones. It starts simply but unlocks deep secrets about the universe.

Why it matters to every student:

🎒 School Level

The Basic Rule: Just add the last two numbers! It's a fun way to practice addition. This exact pattern dictates how many petals grow on flowers, how pinecones spiral, and how shells are shaped!

🎓 College Level

The Golden Ratio: As the numbers grow larger, dividing a Fibonacci number by the previous one converges precisely onto the Golden Ratio (Φ ≈ 1.618). It is vital in computer science algorithms, geometry, and finance.
